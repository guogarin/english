# 0. 单词讲解
## 0.1 mutilate ['mju:tɪleɪt]  
### verb
[动词+名词短语]
**(1) 使残废；使残缺不全；毁伤**
`to damage sb's body very severely, especially by cutting or tearing off part of it`
> The body had been badly mutilated.
> 尸体被严重毁伤。
>
> He was mutilated in a car accident and lost one arm. 
> 
**(2) 严重损毁；毁坏**
`to damage sth very badly`
同义词：`vandalize`
> Intruders slashed and mutilated several paintings.
> 闯进来的人毁坏了好几幅油画。
> 
### 【派生词】`mutilation` 
n. 肢解；（肢体）残缺（毁）；毁伤（形）
> [不可数名词, 可数名词] Thousands suffered death or mutilation in the bomb blast.
> 在炸弹爆炸事件中，数千人被炸死，或成为残废。
> 

`maim` /meɪm/
[动词+名词短语] 使残废；使受重伤
`to injure sb seriously, causing permanent damage to their body`
同义词：`incapacitate`
> Hundreds of people are killed or maimed in car accidents every week.
> 每周都有数百人因车祸而丧命或致残。
> 
> One man has lost his life; another has been maimed. 
> 
`paralyze` 
> The accident left him paralyzed from the waist down. 
> 
`cripple`  
**(1) 使残废；使跛；使成瘸子**
`to damage sb's body so that they are no longer able to walk or move normally`
同义词：`disable`
> He was crippled by polio as a child.
> 他幼年患过小儿麻痹症，结果腿就瘸了。
> 
> to be crippled with arthritis
> 因患关节炎而腿瘸
> 
**(2) 严重毁坏（或损害）**
`to seriously damage or harm sb/sth`
> The pilot tried to land his crippled plane.
> 飞行员试图驾驶损坏严重的飞机着陆。
> 
**(3)【派生词】crippling** 
adj.(疾病等)严重损害健康(或身体)的; 有严重危害的
> a crippling disease
> 严重损害健康的疾病
> 
> crippling debts
> 导致经济瘫痪的债务
> 
`disable`

## 0.2 chew [tʃu:] 
**(1) ~ (at/on/through sth) | ~ sth (up)** 咀嚼；嚼碎
`to bite food into small pieces in your mouth with your teeth to make it easier to swallow`
> [单独使用的动词] After the operation you may find it difficult to chew and swallow.
> 手术后你咀嚼和吞咽都会感到困难。
> 
> [动词+名词短语] teeth designed for chewing meat
> 用于咀嚼肉食的牙齿
> 
> He is always chewing gum.
> 他总是在嚼口香糖。
> 

**(2) ~ (on sth)** （因为紧张等）咬住，不停地啃；（为尝味道）不停地咀嚼
> `to bite sth continuously, for example because you are nervous or to taste it`
> [单独使用的动词] Rosa chewed on her lip and stared at the floor.
> 罗莎咬着嘴唇，眼睛盯着地板。
> 
> The dog was chewing on a bone.
> 那只狗在一个劲地啃骨头。
> 
> [动词+名词短语] to chew your nails
> 啃指甲
> 

**(3) `chew the fat`**
(informal)（长时间）闲聊，闲扯
`to have a long friendly talk with sb about sth`

**(4) `bite off more than you can chew`**
想一口吃成胖子；不自量力
`to try to do too much, or sth that is too difficult`

**(5) `chew sth over`**
仔细考虑；深思熟虑；详细讨论
`to think about or discuss sth slowly and carefully`

> (A) He spilled it on the table. 
> (B) You have an aspirin, don't you? 
> (C) You can sit on the pillow. 
> (D) Swallow this tablet.√ 
> Here, take this pill, and don’t chew it. 
> Answer: (D) Swallow this tablet. 
>
