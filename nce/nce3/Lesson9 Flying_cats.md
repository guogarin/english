## 5. Most cats remain suspicious of humans all their lives.
### 5.1 be suspicious of / about …
#### suspicious
suspicious” 有两层核心含义：
- 一 是“怀疑的、猜疑的”（指人对某事或某人不信任）, 表示“怀疑的、多疑的”（主语是人）
> 译作“怀疑的”“起疑心的”“多疑的”
> She gave me a suspicious look. → 她怀疑地看了我一眼。
> He became suspicious of her motives. → 他对她的动机起了疑心。

> 

- 二是“可疑的、令人起疑的”（指事物或行为本身让人感到不对劲）, 表示“可疑的、令人起疑的”（主语是事物、行为、情况）
> 译作“可疑的”“蹊跷的”“不对劲的”
>  There was a suspicious package on the platform. → 站台上有一个可疑包裹。
>  The police found his story highly suspicious. → 警方觉得他的说法十分蹊跷。
>  It's suspicious that he left so suddenly. → 他走得这么突然，很不对劲。
> 
#### ‘怀疑’的其他表达
`sceptical / skeptical`
> 但和`suspicious`不一样的是，`skeptical`只能表示 **adj.怀疑的**

## 13. 'Cats behave like well-trained paratroopers.' a doctor said. It seems that the further cats fall, the less they are likely to injure themselves.
### 13.1 further
`far` 的比较级有两种形式：`farther`（多用于物理距离）和 `further`（可用于物理距离，也常用于抽象意义如“进一步”）。
**(1) 两种比较级的核心区别**
`farther` 主要用于具体、可测量的物理距离。
> He ran farther than me.（他跑得比我远。） 
> The farther hill is five kilometers away.那座更远的小山在五公里以外。
> 
`further` 可表示物理距离，也可表示抽象意义的“进一步、更多、更深入”。
> We need to further discuss this issue.（我们需要进一步讨论这个问题。） 
> The police decided to investigate further.警方决定作进一步调查。
> 
**(2) 使用建议**
表示物理距离：`farther` / `further` 均可，但美式英语更偏向 `farther`，英式英语更常用 `further`。 
> We had walked further than I had realized.
> = We had walked farther than I had realized.
> 在我不知不觉中我们已走得很远。
> 
表示抽象程度、进展、额外信息：只能用 `further`。 

**(3) 对应最高级**
`farther` → `farthest`（物理距离）
`further` → `furthest`（物理距离或抽象意义） 

#### 13.2 “the + 比较级，the + 比较级”句型和它的变形句型
**翻译要点：不管主句在句尾还是主句在句首，主句所表达的都是越来越的结果的。**
(1) “the + 比较级，the + 比较级”句型比较常见，在通常情况下，前一个“the + 比较级……”是状语从句，后一个“the + 比较级 ……”是主句。此时从句的the + 比较级结构位于从句的句首；主句的比较级结构位于主句之首。
> The faster you run, the harder you breathe.
> 你跑得越快，呼吸就越困难。
> 
> The harder they worked the hungrier they became.
> 条件状语从句，后面的`the hungrier they became`才是主句
>
(2)  “the + 比较级，the + 比较级”的变形句型：即把主句放在整句的句首，把从句放在主句的后面；且主句的比较级总是位于主句的末尾，而且副词the常常可以省略；从句的比较级结构依然位于从句的句首。
> They became (the) hungrier the harder they worked.
> `They became (the) hungrier`是主句
>
> At that moment I tried to relax myself the best I could, because I knew I would use up my air faster the more excited I got.
> 那一刻我竭力放松自己，因为我知道我越是兴奋，空气就越快用完。
>
以下例句是两种句型的相互转化：
> A body weighs less the farther it gets from the surface of the Earth.
> = The farther a body gets from the surface of the Earth, the less it weighs.
> 物体离地球表面越远，它就越轻。
> 
> They became the hungrier the harder they worked.
> = The harder they worked, the hungrier they became.
> 他们干得越起劲就越饿。
> 
> You will speak English better the more you practice. 
> = The more you practise, the better you will speak English.
> 你实操得越多，你英语就会讲得越好。
> 
> You will see farther the higher you climb.
> = The higher you climb, the farther you will see.
> 你爬得越高，看得越远。
> 
新概念中的例句：
> (NCE3-55 P248-L20) A telescope would have to be as far away as the planet Jupiter to look for life in outer space, because the dust becomes thinner the further we travel towards the outer edges of our own solar system.
> 
> The younger the child is, the more is the child's need of sleep.
> 
> The younger the child, the more the child's need of sleep.
> 
> (NCE4-7 P41-L4) The further off this solid obstruction, the longer time will elapse for the return of the echo.
> 
> The further off this solid obstruction is, the longer time will elapse for the return of the echo.
