## 一、单词讲解
## 1. fascinate ['fæsɪneɪt] 
v. 深深吸引；迷住
`to attract or interest sb very much`
> [动词+名词短语] China has always fascinated me.
> 中国一直令我心驰神往。
> 
> It was a question that had fascinated him since he was a boy.
> 这是他自幼就着迷的问题。
> 
> [单独使用的动词] The private lives of movie stars never fail to fascinate.
> 电影明星的私生活总为人所津津乐道。
>
一般使用被动 `be fascinated`
> I am fascinated by the story.
> So the story is fascinating.
> 
### fascinating
adj.迷人的；极有吸引力的
> a fascinating story/subject
> 迷人的故事；趣味无穷的话题
> 
`be  endlessly fascinating`
`find … endlessly fascinating`
> This city is endlessly fascinating.
> 
> I find the natural world endlessly fascinating.
>

n.着迷；魅力；入迷；极大的吸引力
