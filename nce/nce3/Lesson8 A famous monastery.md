# 一、单词讲解

## 1. monastery ['mɑ:nəsteri] 
n. 修道院

- 各类宗教场所对比：
  - **monastery** — 修道院（基督徒）
  - **temple** — 寺庙（佛教）
  - **mosque** — 清真寺（伊斯兰）
  - **nunnery**`/ˈnʌnəri/` — 女修道院
  - **convent**`/ˈkɑːnvent/` — 女修道院

- 男/女修道士：
  - **monk** — 修道士，僧侣
  - **nun**`/nʌn/` — 修女，尼姑
- 和尚们居住和布道都是在一个地方（寺庙），而基督教不一样，修道士住的地方叫`monastery`，但是布道的地方叫：
  - **church** — 教堂，教会
  - **cathedral**`/kəˈθiːdrəl/` — 大教堂（一个教区有很多教堂，这个教区最重要的那个教堂就叫大教堂）

## 2. **St. Bernard [seɪnt-bə:'nɑːd]** 
n. 圣伯纳德（地名）
`St.` → `saint`
`St.` 经常放在一些地名、建筑、大学和节日前面，是为了纪念某位圣徒，比如：
  - St. Andrew's Road 伦敦的圣安德鲁大街
  - St. Paul's Cathedral 伦敦的圣保罗大教堂
  - St. Valentine's Day 圣瓦伦丁节



## 3. rashly ['ræʃlɪ] 
adv. 莽撞地，冒失地
### rash
#### noun
**(1) [可数名词, 通常用单数形式] 皮疹；疹**
`an area of red spots on a person's skin, caused by an illness or a reaction to sth`
> I woke up covered in a rash.
> 我醒来时长了一身皮疹。
> 
> I come out in a rash (= a rash appears on my skin) if I eat chocolate.
> 我一吃巧克力就长皮疹。
> 
> The sun brought her out in (= caused) an itchy rash.
> 太阳晒了她一身痱子。
> 
> a heat rash (= caused by heat) 
> 痱子
> 

**(2) [单数名词] ~ (of sth) 大量；许多；（涌现的）令人不快的事物**
`a lot of sth; a series of unpleasant things that happen over a short period of time`
同义词：`spate`
> a rash of movies about life in prison
> 大量关于监狱生活的电影
> 
> There has been a rash of burglaries in the area over the last month.
> 近一个月这一带发生了很多起入室行窃案。
> 
### adj
`~ (to do sth)`
**of people or their actions 人或行为轻率的；鲁莽的**
`doing sth that may not be sensible without first thinking about the possible results; done in this way`
同义词：`impulsive` , `reckless`
> a rash young man
> 鲁莽的年轻人
> 
> It would be rash to assume that everyone will agree with you on this.
> 你要是认为在这件事上谁都会同意你的看法，那就太欠考虑了。
> 
> Think twice before doing anything rash.
> 不要草率行事，要三思而行。
> 
> This is what happens when you make rash decisions.
> 这就是你贸然作决定的后果。
> 
**同义词**
- **reckless** adj. 鲁莽的，不计后果的
  - He was fined $100 for reckless driving.
- **impulsive** adj. 易冲动的;草率的
  - impulse n. 冲动
  - She's so impulsive—she saw the house for the first time and said she'd buy it straight away.



## 4. enclosure [ɪn'kləʊʒə(r)] 
### noun
**(1) [可数名词] 圈占地；圈用地；围场**
`a piece of land that is surrounded by a fence or wall and is used for a particular purpose`
> a wildlife enclosure
> 野生动物围场
> 
**(2) [不可数名词, 可数名词] 圈地**
`the act of placing a fence or wall around a piece of land`
> the enclosure of common land in the seventeenth century
> 17世纪对公用地的圈占
> 
**(3) [可数名词] （信中）附件**
`something that is placed in an envelope with a letter`
- **enclosure movement** 圈地运动
  - Thomas More — *Utopia*
  - "Sheep eat men."
### **enclose** 
v. 围住；随函(或包裹等)附上；(用墙、篱笆等)把…围起来；附入
be enclosed by …
> The garden was completely enclosed by a high wall.
> 
be enclosed by的近义表达：
- **be surrounded by ...** 被……围绕
> She said that she wanted to die surrounded by the people she loves.
> 
- **be besieged by ...** 被……围困
> Troy was besieged by Greeks.
>

## 5. privacy ['prɪvəsi] / ['praɪvəsi] 
n. 清静，隐私，个人自由

- 常用搭配：protect / respect / invade sb.'s privacy
- **private** adj. 私人的
  - a private conversation / collector

# 第一段

## 1. The Great St. Bernard Pass connects Switzerland to Italy.
### 1.1  connect A with / to B 
把A与B连接起来
> The Channel Tunnel has connected Britain with mainland Europe for the first time.
- 近义表达：
  - **link A with / to B**
  - **join A to B**
> (NCE3-17 P78-L8) The Verrazano Bridge, which was designed by Othmar Ammann, joins Brooklyn to Staten Island.
> 
如果要表示"由A（抽象的）联系、关联到了B"，可以用：
  - **connect A with / to B**
  - **link A with / to B**
  - 不用 ~~join A to B~~
> There is no evidence to **link / connect** him with the murder.
> 
另外还可以用下面两个表示由A（抽象的）联系、关联到了B，但是下面两个不能在物理上将两个东西连起来：
  - **associate A with B**
  - **relate A to B**
> (NCE3-23 P114-L10) There are countless people who, ever since their early years, have learned to associate snails with food.
> 

### 1.2 The Great St. Bernard Pass
在这里pass作名词表示：
> n. 关口；关隘；山路
> 
`a road or way over or through mountains`
> a mountain pass
> 山口
>

### 1.3 Switzerland的发音
`Switzerland`/ˈswitsə(r)ˌlənd/
`pizza`/ˈpiːtsə/ 
`Nazi`/ˈnɑːtsi/
这三个单词中间都有音标`ts`，这个英标有点类似于 “刺”

### 2.1 At 2,473 metres
- **at …** 表示"在某个高度/数值"
在这句话里 `at`=`at an altitude of …`
> Being at 2,473 meters, ...
> 

### 2.2 造句练习：  - 4807 米的勃朗峰 (Mont Blanc) 是阿尔卑斯山脉 (the Alps) 的最高峰。
At 4,807 meters, Mont Blanc is the highest peak of the Alps.
`Mont Blanc`来自法语



## 3. The famous monastery of St. Bernard, which was founded in the eleventh century, lies about a mile away.
### 3.1 the + 名词 + of + 专有名词 结构
**the + 名词 + of + 专有名词**引出地点的名字
> (NCE3-3 P22-L2) the Aegean island of Kea
> 
> (NCE3-3 P22-L3) the promontory of Ayia Irini
> 
> (NCE3-16 P74-L1) the tiny village of Perachora
>

> the novel of *Three Kingdoms* ✗
> the novel entitled *Three Kingdoms* ✓
> the movie entitled *Da Vinci Code*
> the man named / called Vincent
>

### 3.2 was founded in the eleventh century
表示"始建于、追溯到"：
> (NCE3-3 P22-L11) The body of one statue was found among remains dating from the fifteenth century B.C.
> 
  - **date from …**
  - **date back to …**
  - **can be traced back to …**
  - 句型转换：
    - …, which dates from the 11th century, …
    - …, which dates back to the 11th century, …
    - …, which can be traced back to the 11th century, …
### 3.3 lies about a mile away
表示"坐落于、位于，还有其他的表达
> (NCE3-3 P22-L2) An American team explored a temple which **stands** in an ancient city on the promontory of Ayia Irini.
> 
stand一般用于比较小的单个物体（比如一座庙），lie一般用于比较大的物体（比如一座圣伯纳德修道院，它是一个建筑群）



## 4. For hundreds of years, St. Bernard dogs have saved the lives of travellers crossing the dangerous Pass.
### 4.1  St. Bernard Dog 
圣伯纳犬

### 4.2 表达"以某种方式穿越"：
本文：
> (NCE3-8 P42-L10) cross the pass on foot
> (NCE3-8 P42-L11) cross the pass in cars
> 
  - **cross + 宾语 + 方式状语**
- 例句拓展：
  - (NCE3-12 P58-L11) They quickly loaded a small rubber dinghy with food, matches, and tins of beer and rowed for a few miles across the Caribbean until they arrived at a tiny coral island.
    - row across the Caribbean
  - (NCE3-20 P90-L1) In 1908 Lord Northcliffe offered a prize of ￡1000 to the first man who would fly across the English Channel.
    - fly across the English Channel
- **动词 + across + 宾语** 结构：
  - cross the pass on foot = walk across the pass
  - cross the pass in cars = drive across the pass
  - row across the Caribbean = cross the Caribbean by boat



## 5. These friendly dogs, which were first brought from Asia, were used as watchdogs even in Roman times.

- 复合形容词：
  - environment-friendly 环保的
  - ozone-friendly 对臭氧层无害的
- **bring … from …** 从……带来……
- **introduce … from …** 从……引进……
  - ..., which were introduced from Asia, …
  - The grey squirrel was introduced into Britain from North America.
  - (NCE4-17 P98-L1) In the early days of the settlement of Australia, enterprising settlers unwisely introduced the European rabbit.

## 6. Now that a tunnel has been built through the mountains, the Pass is less dangerous, but each year, the dogs are still sent out into the snow whenever a traveller is in difficulty.

- **now (that)** conj. 同时表示时间和原因（既然）
  - Now (that) you are a big boy you must behave better.
- **tunnel** n. 隧道，地道
  - the Channel Tunnel 英吉利海峡隧道
- **canal** n. 运河
  - the Panama Canal 巴拿马运河

- **疑问词 + ever 的含义**
  1. **表示"到底……"** — 引导疑问句
     - (NCE3-2 P18-L10) 'Whatever are you doing up here Bill?' asked the vicar in surprise.
  2. **表示"无论……"** — 引导让步状语从句 = no matter + 疑问词
     - (NCE3-1 P14-L11) Wherever it went, it left behind it a trail of dead deer and small animals like rabbits.
     - No matter where it went, it left behind it a trail of dead deer and small animals like rabbits.
     - …, the dogs are still sent out into the snow no matter when a traveller is in difficulty.

- **知新**："疑问词+ever"表示"无论"时还可以用于**名词性从句**，此时 ≠ "no matter+疑问词"（后者只能用于让步状语从句）
  - Whoever wants to sign up for the course is welcome. ✓
  - No matter who wants to sign up for the course is welcome. ✗
  - No matter who wants to sign up for the course, he is welcome. ✓
  - Whatever project he works on is always successful.
  - No matter what project he works on, it is always successful.

  - (NCE3-20 P90-L2) Over a year passed before the first attempt was made.
  - (NCE3-38 P174-L21) It seems that man was making a real effort to understand the seasons 20,000 years earlier than has been supposed.
  - (NCE4-24 P138-L14) … if, in a word, beauty means something, yet we must not seek to interpret the meaning.

# 第二段

## 8. During the summer months, the monastery is very busy, for it is visited by thousands of people who cross the Pass in cars.

- **in cars = by car** 乘汽车
- 表示"使用某种交通工具"的介词短语规则：
  - **"by"** 后要直接加单数名词
  - **"in"** 后的名词要有人称和数的变化
  - You can go to the airport in my car.
  - We crossed the river in a boat.

## 9. As there are so many people about, the dogs have to be kept in a special enclosure.

- 句型转换：
  - As there are so many people about, the dogs have to be …
  - **(With) so many people (being) about**, the dogs have to be …
- **keep** v. = confine 把……关起来
  - He was kept / confined in prison.
  - You shouldn't keep / confine the bird in a cage.

## 10. In winter, however, life at the monastery is quite different.

- **life at the monastery** 修道院的生活
  - life on a desert island 荒岛生活
  - life in the army 军旅生活
  - life on campus 校园生活
- **quite** adv. = completely 完全，截然
  - (NCE3-12 P58-L4) The other side of the picture is quite the opposite.
  - (NCE3-30 P142-L21) He used to sleep during the day and work at night, quite unaware of the fact that he had become the ghost of Endley.

## 11. The temperature drops to -30° and very few people attempt to cross the Pass.

- 表示"温度下降"：
  - **drop** v.
  - **fall** v.
    - The temperature falls to -30°…
    - (NCE3-50 P228-L24) Little by little the eleven minutes fell to zero.
  - **plunge** v. 暴跌
    - (NCE3-10 P50-L15) The order to abandon ship was given and hundreds of people plunged into the icy water.
    - The temperature plunges to -30°…
  - **dive to ...** 骤降
- 表示"温度上升"：
  - increase to ...
  - rise to ...
  - rocket to ...
  - soar to ...
- 注意区别：
  - The temperature drops **by** 10°. （下降了……）
  - The temperature drops **to** -30°. （下降到……）

- **-30° 的读法**：
  - minus 30 degrees
  - 30 degrees below zero
  - -30°C → minus 30 degrees Centigrade / Celsius
  - -30°F → minus 30 degrees Fahrenheit

## 12. The monks prefer winter to summer for they have more privacy.

- **prefer A to B** 喜欢A胜过B
  - I prefer mutton to beef.
- **prefer doing to doing**
  - I prefer standing to sitting down.
- 听力例题：
  - (A) She doesn't like to go shopping. ✓
  - (B) She went shopping yesterday.
  - (C) She doesn't live near the shops.
  - (D) She prefers shopping to studying.
  - **W**: Didn't Marian go shopping with you yesterday?
  - **M**: Even if she hadn't had a lot of studying, she would have preferred staying home to going shopping.
  - **Q**: What does the man imply about Marian?
  - **Answer: (A) She doesn't like to go shopping.**

- 其他表示"偏好"的句型：
  - **prefer A rather than B**
    - I prefer mutton rather than beef.
  - **prefer to do rather than do**
    - I prefer to stand rather than sit down.
  - **would rather do than do**
    - I'd rather stand than sit down.
    - (NCE3-14 P66-L7) … people would rather pay large sums of money than have their life work destroyed by gangsters.

- 听力例题 2：
  - (A) She wants the man to be at the station when she arrives.
  - (B) She isn't sure which train she'll be on. ✓
  - (C) The train will be an hour late.
  - (D) She'll leave home at 6:30.
  - **M**: It says here the next train is due in at 6:30.
  - **W**: I know, but I don't know whether I can make that one. I'd rather call you from the station than have you waiting around for an hour.
  - **Q**: What does the woman imply?
  - **Answer: (B) She isn't sure which train she'll be on.**

## 13. The dogs have greater freedom, too, for they are allowed to wander outside their enclosure.

- 表示"允许某人做某事"：
  - permit sb. to do
  - allow sb. to do
  - give sb. permission to do
- **wander** v. = roam 漫步，闲逛（to walk aimlessly）

## 14. The only regular visitors to the monastery in winter are parties of skiers who go there at Christmas and Easter.

- **regular** n. 常客
  - He is one of our restaurant's regulars.
  - What'll it be? — The usual, please.
- **a party of** 一群，一队
  - a party of tourists 一群游客
  - a search party 搜索队
  - a rescue party 救援队
- 表示"一群"的词：group / bunch / crowd / band
  - (NCE3-14 P66-L9) Six hundred years ago, Sir John Hawkwood arrived in Italy with a band of soldiers and settled near Florence.
- 节日前的介词：
  - on Christmas Day
  - on Easter Day
  - on / at weekends

## 15. These young people, who love the peace of mountains, always receive a warm welcome at St. Bernard's monastery.

- 近义表达：
  - …, are always **warmly welcomed** ….
  - **receive a warm welcome** 受到热烈欢迎
  - Let's all extend a warm welcome to our visitors.
- **receive + n.** 受到；得到；遭到
  - (NCE3-23 P114-L9) No creature has received more praise and abuse than the common garden snail.
  - receive insults 受到侮辱
  - receive support 得到支持
  - receive adequate treatment 得到充分治疗
  - receive good education 接受良好教育
  - (NCE3-31 P146-L20) This exhibition received a great deal of attention in the press, …
